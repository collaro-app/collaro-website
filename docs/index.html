For dog owners

Everything you need to find and book care

Whether it's a weekend of boarding or a daily walk, Collaro helps you find the right provider and manage the booking from start to finish.

  • Discover services near you

    Browse providers on a map, search by name, city or street, and filter by service type, provider type, rating, completed bookings or verified status.

  • Explore provider profiles

    Photos, an about section, the services they offer, and ratings and reviews from other dog owners.

  • Prices and availability up front

    See each service's pricing and schedule, choose dates and times that are actually free, and get the price before you send a request.

  • Book in a few taps

    Choose your dog, your dates and times, add a note for the provider, and send a booking request. The provider accepts and you're notified.

  • Chat with your provider

    Once a booking is accepted, message each other directly in the app.

  • Manage bookings and dogs in one place

    Keep your dogs' profiles, upcoming and past bookings, invoices and reviews together — and leave a review once a booking is complete and paid.

For providers

Offer your services and manage bookings from your phone

Boarding, home sitting, dog walking, grooming or training — Collaro gives you a profile, a booking inbox and the tools to run your day.

  • Create your provider profile

    Add photos, describe what you offer, pin your location, and present yourself as a business or a private provider.

  • Publish your services

    List any of the five service types with a description and how many dogs you can take at once.

  • Set your prices and availability

    Weekly schedules or specific dates, working hours, and flat or size-based pricing — you decide, and Collaro shows it to owners.

  • Receive and manage bookings

    Accept or decline requests, see each dog's details and the owner's notes, and keep track of upcoming and completed bookings.

  • Chat with dog owners

    Once you accept a booking, message the owner directly in the app.

  • Build your presence

    Collect ratings and reviews from completed bookings, showcase your gallery, and stand out with a Verified badge after an optional identity check (a PRO feature).

Free to start

Every provider starts on the Free plan. Owners pay you directly — Collaro doesn't take a commission or add fees to your bookings. A PRO plan with higher limits and extra tools is available; contact us to learn more.

How it works

From “I need a dog sitter” to booked, in four steps

  1. 1

    Discover

    Open the map, search or filter, and find providers near you.

  2. 2

    Explore

    Compare profiles, services, prices, ratings and reviews.

  3. 3

    Request

    Pick your dog, dates and times, see the price, and send a booking request.

  4. 4

    Get going

    The provider accepts, you're notified, and you can chat in the app. You arrange payment directly with the provider.

Why Collaro

Dog care, without the back-and-forth

Finding dog care usually means scattered messages, unclear prices and a lot of guesswork. Collaro brings owners and providers together in one place.

  • Everything in one place

    Discovery, profiles, availability, booking requests, messages and reviews — one app instead of a dozen chats.

  • Clear before you commit

    Service details, pricing, availability, ratings and reviews are visible before you send a request — and nothing is booked until the provider accepts.

  • Direct and simple

    You and your provider talk directly in the app and arrange payment between you. Collaro doesn't add fees or commission to bookings.

  • Built for both sides

    Owners get a simple way to find care; providers get a profile, a booking inbox and tools to manage their work — in English and Hebrew, on iOS and Android.
