We’re here to help

Support

Questions, problems with your account, a booking issue, a privacy request, or anything else — email us and a human will get back to you.

contact@collaro.app

We read every message and get back to you as soon as we can.

Email support

You can write to us in English or Hebrew.

To help us answer faster, please include:

  • The email address of your Collaro account
  • Whether you're a dog owner or a service provider
  • A short description of the issue (and a screenshot, if you have one)

From inside the app

Open the side menu and tap Contact us to send us a message from your account.

Deleting your account or data

You can delete (deactivate) your account from inside the app: open the side menu → Settings → scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account. Deleting the account in the app keeps its data so that you can restore it later. To have your personal information removed completely — or if you can no longer sign in — email us from the address registered to your account and we'll handle it for you.
