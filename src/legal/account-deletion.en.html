Account Deletion

Last updated: 24 September 2026

This page explains how to delete your Collaro account in the app, how to ask us to remove your personal information completely, and which records we keep. It summarises, for convenience, what our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service say about account deletion; where this page and those documents differ, those documents apply.

1. Two Different Things

  • Deleting your account in the app deactivates it. The account is hidden and can no longer be used, but its data is kept so that you can restore it later. The app does not erase your data.
  • Asking us to remove your personal information completely is a separate request, which our support team handles manually — see “Asking Us to Remove Your Personal Information Completely” below.

2. How to Delete Your Account in the App

You can delete your Collaro account yourself, at any time, from inside the app:

  1. Open the Collaro app and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Open the side menu using the menu button in the top bar.
  3. Tap Settings.
  4. At the bottom of the screen, below Sign out, tap Delete account.
  5. Read the confirmation screen — it tells you whether any of your bookings prevent deletion and which pending requests will be cancelled — and confirm.

Deleting the account signs you out on every device immediately.

3. When Deletion Is Not Available Yet

To protect commitments that are already in progress, deletion is not available while any of your bookings is accepted and not yet concluded. Cancelling an accepted booking normally requires the other party’s approval, so please wait for those bookings to complete, or cancel them by mutual agreement, first.

When the deletion does go through:

  • Any pending booking requests that have not yet been accepted are cancelled automatically.
  • Any open payment-confirmation request you are involved in is closed.
  • A PRO period on your account is not renewed; it simply runs to its end date.

4. What Happens to Your Account and Your Data

Deleting your account in the app deactivates it rather than erasing it. While it is deactivated, the account cannot sign in or act normally, it receives no notifications (other than an account-restoration code you ask for), and your profile, dogs and services are not shown to other users.

We keep the data on the account — your name and email address, your dogs and their photos, your provider profile and its images, your services, your identity-verification result, your profile photo, your reviews and your messages — so that the account can be restored later. We do not delete deactivated accounts automatically.

Records that also belong to someone else stay available to them, without your name:

  • On a past booking, its invoice or an earlier conversation, you are shown as a generic “deleted user”. If you are an Owner, your dogs’ names, notes, medical notes and photos are hidden from the Provider.
  • Reviews you wrote stay public on the Provider’s profile, attributed to a “deleted user”, and continue to count towards the Provider’s rating.

5. Restoring Your Account

To restore your account, sign in with your existing credentials and verify a one-time code we email to your account address. Verifying it re-activates the account with your data intact.

6. Asking Us to Remove Your Personal Information Completely

If you want your personal information removed completely rather than deactivated, or you would like a copy of it, contact our support team:

  • by email at contact@collaro.app, from the address on your Collaro account; or
  • in the app, through Contact us in the side menu.

We handle these requests manually. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request, and we will respond within the time required by applicable law. We then delete your personal information, except for the records described in “Records We Keep” below.

Contact us by email in the same way if you can no longer sign in, or if you started signing up but did not finish — for example, you did not verify your email address or did not choose a role. The app cannot delete such an account itself, so we will delete it for you.

7. Records We Keep

Some records describe something that happened between you and another user, and are that person’s record as much as yours. To keep other users’ records complete and consistent, we keep them even after an account is deleted or its personal information is removed:

  • past bookings and their invoices;
  • reviews you wrote;
  • messages you sent, which remain in the other person’s conversation;
  • reports you filed or that concern you, together with their evidence.

To other users you appear in these records only as a generic “deleted user”. We also keep our internal audit record of booking-, plan- and account-related events, which refers to accounts by internal identifiers, and we may retain other information where necessary to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent fraud and abuse, and enforce our agreements.

When an image is deleted, we make a best effort to delete the corresponding file from our image host; copies may persist for a period in caches outside our control, and externally hosted images (such as a Google profile picture) are not stored or deleted by us.

8. Deleting Only Part of Your Data

You do not have to close your account to remove content you have added. Dogs, services and gallery images can be deleted individually from the app — see Deleting Your Data.
