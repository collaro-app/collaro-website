Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026 · Version 1.0

This Privacy Policy explains how Collaro (the “Platform”, “we”, “us” or “our”), operated by Ofek Levi, collects, uses, stores, shares and protects your personal information when you use our mobile application and related services that connect dog owners with independent care providers offering boarding, home sitting, dog walking, grooming and training. By creating an account or using the Platform, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Policy. If you do not agree with it, please do not use the Platform.

1. About This Policy and the Platform

Collaro is an online marketplace that helps dog owners (“Owners”) discover and book independent care providers (“Providers”). We provide the technology that connects Owners and Providers; we do not provide dog-care services ourselves and we do not employ Providers.

This Policy applies to all users of the Platform, including Owners, Providers and administrators, and covers information we process through the mobile application, our application programming interfaces (APIs) and related communications such as email and push notifications.

Capitalised terms that are not defined in this Policy have the meaning given to them in our Terms of Service.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the information described below so that the Platform can function, so that Owners and Providers can find and trust one another, and so that bookings can be arranged and managed.

  • Account information: your name, your email address, the role you choose (Owner or Provider), your preferred language, your email-verification status, your notification preference, an optional profile photo, and the version of the Terms of Service and this Policy that you accepted. If you register with an email and password, we store your password only as a one-way cryptographic hash (bcrypt); we never store it in readable form.
  • Google sign-in information: if you continue with Google, we receive and store a Google account identifier, your name and email address from Google, and a link to your Google profile picture (which remains hosted by Google and is not copied to our storage; it is used as your profile photo unless you upload your own). We do not receive your Google password. Your account is created when you first continue with Google, before you choose your role and accept the Terms of Service and this Policy.
  • Email-verification, password-reset and account-restoration codes: we generate a one-time code and store only a hashed version of it; the code itself is sent to your email address and is never retained after it is used, replaced or expires.
  • Provider profile information (Providers only): your public display name (if you do not set one, your account name is shown instead), whether you offer your services as a business or as a private individual, a phone number, a postal address (street, city and country), a precise map location (geographic coordinates) for your service point and the time zone derived from it, a written biography, and an optional image gallery. For each service you offer, we also store its type, description, capacity, availability schedule and prices.
  • Dog information (Owners): for each dog you add — its name, breed, age, size, gender, optional free-text notes, whether you declare it vaccinated and healthy, and, where it is not, free-text medical notes. You may also add a photo of each dog. Medical and health notes are sensitive information; see “Sensitive Information”.
  • Identity-verification data (Providers): if you choose to use the verification feature, you submit an image of a government-issued identification document and a selfie. These images are sent to our AI provider (OpenAI) for an automated check and are not stored by us — we retain only the outcome (verified or not), the reasons for any rejection, and the time of the check. See “Identity Verification”.
  • Booking information: the bookings you create or receive, including the selected service, the booking’s dates and times and the time zone in which they are scheduled, the dogs involved (with a copy of each dog’s profile as it was when the request was sent), any notes the Owner writes for the Provider, the price and how it was calculated, the parties, the booking status, and the booking’s history — including any cancellation requests (with the reason given, if any) and the recording and confirmation of the direct payment.
  • Plan information (Providers only): your plan (Free or PRO), its status and dates, whether a PRO period was granted by us, and a counter of bookings accepted in the current period. Online payments are not available, so we do not collect payment-card details; payment for bookings is always arranged directly between the Owner and the Provider and never passes through the Platform (see “Payments”).
  • Messages and support requests: the content and metadata (sender, timestamps, read status) of messages you exchange with another user or with our support team through the in-app chat, and the messages you send us through the Contact us form in the app, together with the reason you choose and your name, email address, role and account identifier, which are included so that we can reply to you.
  • Reviews and ratings: the star ratings and written reviews you submit about a Provider, and the reviews submitted about you as a Provider.
  • Reports: if you report a Provider, the reason you select, your written description and any evidence photographs you attach.
  • Device and technical information: a per-installation device identifier generated on your device, push-notification tokens for your devices, authentication tokens that keep you signed in, the time each of your sign-in sessions started and was last used, and app settings such as your chosen language and display theme (the theme is stored only on your device). When your device connects to our servers, they also process its IP address (see “How We Protect Your Information”).
  • Location information: a Provider’s chosen service coordinates (stored as part of the provider profile). For Owners, if you allow location access, your device’s location is read when you open the map and is sent to our servers to show nearby Providers; it is not stored on our servers. See “Location Information”.
  • Moderation information: if we block a Provider account, we keep a record of the block, when it was made and the reason given.

Providing this information is your choice, but some of it is needed to use the Platform — for example, an email address to create an account, and a phone number, address, service location and biography to be listed as a Provider. If you do not provide it, you will not be able to use the features that depend on it.

4. Sensitive Information

Some information you provide is sensitive, and we want to be clear about how it is handled.

  • Dog health and medical notes: if you mark a dog as not healthy, you are asked to describe its condition. These free-text health notes, together with the rest of the dog’s profile, are disclosed to a Provider when you send that Provider a booking request — that is, before the Provider accepts. A Provider you request will therefore see this information even if they later decline or do not respond. The booking keeps a copy of the dog’s profile as it was when you sent the request, and that copy remains visible to the Provider as part of the booking record, even if you later edit or delete the dog, unless you delete your account (see “Account Deletion and Data Retention”). Only include details you are comfortable sharing with the Provider you are requesting.
  • Identity documents and biometric data: the ID image and selfie you submit to the verification feature are sent to our AI provider (OpenAI), which analyses them to check the document and whether the selfie plausibly matches it. This may involve automated facial analysis, performed solely to compute the result. We do not store the images, and they are never shown to other users. Under OpenAI’s API data policies they are not used to train its models, but OpenAI may retain them for a limited period (by default, up to 30 days) for abuse monitoring — see “Identity Verification”.

5. How and Why We Use Identity Verification

Identity verification is a feature of the PRO plan that lets a Provider display a “Verified” badge; during Early Access it is available to every Provider (see the Terms of Service, “Provider Plans”). You are never required to verify your identity to browse or use the Platform, and a badge you have already earned remains on your profile regardless of your plan.

If you choose to use it, you submit an image of a government-issued identification document together with a selfie. Our server runs an automated check and returns a decision. We want to be transparent about what this feature is and is not:

  • The check is fully automated and is performed by our AI provider (OpenAI). We send it your ID image and selfie, and it assesses whether the document appears to be a national ID, passport, or driver's license and is readable, whether the selfie is a clear photo of a single real person, and whether the two plausibly show the same person. The badge is granted only when these automated checks pass.
  • It is NOT a full KYC process, a government-database check, a document-authenticity or anti-forgery check, or a “liveness”/anti-spoofing check, and it does not involve manual human review. It can make mistakes in both directions. The “Verified” badge means our automated checks passed — not that we have independently confirmed your legal identity.
  • Your ID image and selfie are sent to OpenAI solely to perform this check and are not stored by us. OpenAI processes them to compute the result; under its API data policies it does not use them to train its models, but it may retain them for a limited period (by default, up to 30 days) for abuse monitoring. We retain only the result (approved or rejected), the reasons for any rejection, and the time of the check.
  • Because we do not keep the images, they are never shown to other users; only your verification status (verified or not) is visible to others.
  • We do not sell or rent this data, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.
  • The verification result stored on your account is kept if you delete your account in the app (so the account can be restored) and is removed if you ask us to remove your personal information completely, as described in “Account Deletion and Data Retention”.

6. How Information Is Shared and What Other Users See

The Platform is a marketplace, so some information is shared with other users so that bookings can take place. We share only what is necessary, and we never sell your personal information.

  • Public Provider information: once a Provider is listed, their display name (or, if none is set, their account name), profile photo, whether they are a business or a private provider, biography, gallery images, services with their prices, schedules and remaining availability, time zone, average rating and reviews, number of completed bookings and verification status — and also their phone number, full street address and exact map location (shown on the map as a pin, together with a distance from the centre of the map being viewed) — are publicly accessible, including to people who do not have an account (for example through our public interfaces). Providers should bear this in mind when choosing the address and location pin they publish.
  • Reviews: reviews and ratings are public and are attributed to the Owner who wrote them, including that Owner’s name and profile photo (for an account created with Google, the name and picture from Google). If the Owner later deletes their account, the review stays public but is attributed to a generic “deleted user”.
  • Information shared with your booking counterpart: when an Owner sends a booking request, the Owner’s name and profile photo, any notes written for the Provider, and the selected dogs’ full profiles (including any health and medical notes) are shared with that Provider. Once the booking is accepted, the parties can exchange messages in the app. A reason given with a cancellation request is shown to the other party, and the invoice for a booking shows both parties’ names and the names of the dogs.
  • Information visible to administrators: our administrators can access account details (such as name, email address, role and profile photo), reports (including the booking and service they concern), Providers’ plan and block details, and our internal audit record of booking-, plan- and account-related events, in order to operate the Platform and moderate reports. Messages you send through the Contact us form reach our support team by email. Administrators cannot read messages exchanged between Owners and Providers; they can only see messages in support conversations they take part in.
  • Information not shown to other users: your password, your email address, your identity-verification images (which are sent to our AI provider for the check and are not retained by us), your IP address, your plan details, and an Owner’s device location.
  • Service providers (sub-processors): we use the trusted third parties listed under “Third-Party Services” to host our servers and data, store images, send email and push notifications, provide maps and geocoding, and perform the automated identity-verification check. They process information only as needed to provide their service to us.
  • Legal and safety disclosures: we may disclose information where required by law, to enforce our Terms, or to protect the rights, property or safety of our users, the public or the Platform.

7. Payments

Payment for bookings is never processed by the Platform. The price of a booking is agreed and settled directly between the Owner and the Provider — for example in cash — according to their own arrangement. The Platform does not process, hold, transfer or collect any of that money and stores no payment instrument for it; it only provides a two-sided confirmation in which one party records that the payment was made and the other confirms it. That confirmation is a record only and does not move any money.

No payments are made through the Platform. Online payments are not available: the optional PRO plan cannot be purchased in the app, no payment card can be entered or saved, and we do not collect any payment-card details (see the Terms of Service, “Provider Plans”).

If we introduce online payments in the future, they will be handled by one or more third-party payment processors, and we will update this Policy before any payment is processed.

8. Third-Party Services

We rely on a limited number of third-party services to operate the Platform. They act as our service providers and may process your information to provide their service to us; their own processing, where they act as independent controllers, is governed by their respective privacy policies.

  • Render — hosts our servers, including our application programming interface and real-time messaging, and stores our servers’ operational logs, which record limited technical information such as account identifiers and the recipient address and subject of emails we send.
  • Database and data-store hosting — our MongoDB database, which holds the Platform’s data (such as accounts, profiles, dogs, services, bookings, messages, reviews and reports), and a Redis data store, which holds short-lived operational data (such as rate-limiting counters and map-search results cached for a few minutes) and relays real-time updates between our servers, are hosted by cloud service providers.
  • Cloudinary — image storage and content delivery for uploaded images (profile photos, dog photos, provider galleries and report evidence). Uploaded images are served from content-delivery links that are not individually access-restricted, which means anyone who has an image’s link can view that image. Identity-verification images are NOT uploaded to Cloudinary — they are sent to our AI provider for the check and then discarded (see “Identity Verification”).
  • OpenAI — used only for the optional identity-verification feature: when a Provider submits an ID image and a selfie, those images are sent to OpenAI to perform the automated check. We do not store them; under OpenAI’s API data policies they are not used to train its models and may be retained by OpenAI for a limited period (by default, up to 30 days) for abuse monitoring (see “Identity Verification”).
  • Google — used to authenticate you if you choose “Continue with Google” (Google confirms your identity to us and provides basic profile information: identifier, name, email and a profile-picture link; that picture is displayed to other users directly from Google’s servers) and, together with Waze, as an optional external navigation app (see below).
  • Waze and Google Maps — when you ask for directions to a Provider or to a dog park, the app opens one of these navigation apps (or its website) on your device and passes the destination coordinates to it; your use of that app is then governed by that app’s own privacy policy.
  • Resend (email delivery) — used to send verification and account codes, completed-service summaries, account and plan notices, and support messages, and to deliver the messages you send through the Contact us form to our support inbox.
  • Expo push notification service — used to deliver push notifications to your devices using device push tokens; it receives the content of the notifications we send you.
  • Photon and Nominatim (OpenStreetMap-based geocoding services) — used, via our servers, to turn text you type into an address or place search into places and coordinates, and to turn coordinates into an address (for example when a Provider uses their current location to set a service address).
  • Overpass API (an OpenStreetMap-based service) — used, via our servers, to look up public dog parks near a map location; your approximate (coarsened) map-center coordinates are sent to it for this query.
  • CARTO and a public code-delivery network (unpkg) — used to display interactive maps based on OpenStreetMap data: your device fetches the mapping library from unpkg and map tiles for the area being viewed from CARTO, so these services receive your device’s IP address and, through the tiles requested, can see the area you are viewing.
  • Apple Push Notification service and Firebase Cloud Messaging — used only as the delivery transport for push notifications on iOS and Android devices respectively (not for analytics).

We do not use advertising networks, and we do not use analytics, telemetry, crash-reporting or product-tracking SDKs in the application.

9. How Uploaded Images Are Handled

Most images you upload — profile photos, dog photos, provider gallery images, and report evidence — are stored with our image hosting provider, Cloudinary, and are delivered from public content-delivery links. This means those images may be viewable by anyone who has the direct link, even if the surrounding content is not otherwise public. Identity-verification images are the exception: they are never uploaded to our image host — they are sent to our AI provider for the automated check and then discarded (see “Identity Verification”).

We do not currently remove embedded metadata (such as any location or device data your camera may store) from images before they are uploaded. If you do not wish to share such metadata, please remove it before uploading or disable it on your device.

When you delete or replace an image, we make a best effort to delete the corresponding file from our image host, with two exceptions: the photos of a dog that has been included in a booking are kept, because the booking keeps a copy of the dog’s profile, and report evidence is kept with the report. Copies may also persist for a period in caches outside our control, and externally hosted images (such as a Google profile picture) are not stored or deleted by us. When you delete your account in the app, your uploaded images are retained so the account can be restored (see “Account Deletion and Data Retention”).

10. Location Information

  • Providers: the service location you set is stored as part of your profile at the precision you choose, is shown publicly as a map pin and used to calculate distances, and is used to determine the time zone in which your services are scheduled and your bookings’ dates and times are shown. We do not blur or approximate it, so choose your pin accordingly. If you choose to use your device’s current location when setting your service address, that location is sent via our servers to our geocoding provider to look up the matching address.
  • Owners: the map is the Owner home screen. When it opens, the app asks for permission to use your location (unless you have already answered) and, if you allow it, reads your device’s current location in the foreground — each time the map opens and whenever you tap the location button — to centre the map and find nearby Providers. The location is sent to our servers to search for nearby Providers; it is used in the moment and is not stored on our servers. If you decline the location permission, the map simply opens at a default area.

We do not track your location in the background, build a location history, or use your location for any purpose other than the map, search, distance and time-zone features described above.

11. Cookies, Tokens and On-Device Storage

The Platform is a mobile application and does not use browser cookies. To keep you signed in and to operate the app, we use authentication tokens and on-device storage.

On your device, your sign-in tokens are stored in the operating system’s secure keystore.

We also store, in ordinary on-device storage, a per-installation device identifier, your preferences (such as language and display theme), your device’s push token and a few display settings (for example, whether you have dismissed a notice). The device identifier is generated on your device, is used to keep one active session per device, and is not a hardware identifier or advertising identifier. Signing out and clearing the app’s storage removes this locally stored information from your device; files you choose to export or save (such as an invoice) remain where you saved them until you delete them.

12. Notifications and Communications

We send two kinds of messages. Emails are sent for things you may need a record of, and cannot be switched off while you have an account: your verification, password-reset and account-restoration codes; the completed-service summary sent to both parties when a booking ends; messages from our support team (the full text of support-conversation messages may be delivered to you by email); and, for Providers, notices when we block or unblock your account (including the reason given). Push notifications carry your day-to-day activity: booking requests and their acceptance, rejection or expiry, cancellations and cancellation requests, payment confirmations, new reviews and new chat messages. Push notifications are optional, and you can turn them on or off with a single switch in Settings. Apart from the completed-service summary, which is also emailed, these updates are delivered by push only, so turning push off (or declining the permission) means you will not be notified of them outside the app — they remain visible in the app at any time. There is currently no separate per-category control, and we do not send marketing or promotional notifications.

Push notifications may include limited details, such as a counterpart’s name, a booking date or a star rating; they never include your verification codes or the content of chat messages. Depending on your device settings, the content of a notification may be shown on your lock screen.

Your information is stored in our database (MongoDB) and, for images, with our image hosting provider (Cloudinary); our servers are hosted by Render, and short-lived operational data is kept in a Redis data store (see “Third-Party Services”). These providers, and others listed there such as OpenAI, Resend and Expo, may process your information in countries other than your country of residence, including the United States.

14. How We Protect Your Information

We apply technical and organisational measures designed to protect your information, including: storing passwords only as one-way bcrypt hashes; storing verification codes and session refresh tokens only in hashed form; short-lived access tokens with rotating, revocable refresh sessions; authentication, role and ownership checks on access to data; rate limiting and security headers; limits on upload size and type; and configuration checks that prevent the production service from starting with insecure secrets. Our rate limiting briefly processes your IP address and, for some actions, your account identifier or email address, in counters that expire automatically (typically within an hour). In our production environment, connections between the app and our servers are protected with transport encryption (HTTPS/TLS).

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. As noted above, uploaded images are served from public links. You are responsible for keeping your password and device secure and for activity that occurs under your account.

15. Account Deletion and Data Retention

Deleting your account in the app. You can delete your account at any time from Settings. Deleting your account in the app deactivates it; it does not erase your data. To protect commitments in progress, deletion is not available while any of your bookings is accepted and not yet concluded — cancelling an accepted booking normally requires the other party’s approval, so please wait for those bookings to complete, or cancel them by mutual agreement, first. When you delete your account, any pending booking requests that have not yet been accepted are automatically cancelled, any open payment-confirmation request you are involved in is closed, you are signed out on all your devices, and a PRO period on your account is not renewed and simply runs to its end date.

What deactivation keeps. When you delete your account in the app, we keep your data — including your name and email address, your dogs and their photos, your provider profile and its images, your services, your identity-verification result, your profile photo, your reviews, your messages and your devices’ push tokens — so that the account can be restored later. While your account is deleted it is deactivated: it cannot sign in or act normally, it receives no notifications (other than an account-restoration code you request), and your profile, dogs and services are not shown to other users. Records that also belong to someone else remain available to them without your name: on a past booking, its invoice or a previous conversation you are shown as a generic “deleted user” (and, if you are an Owner, your dogs’ names, notes, medical notes and photos are hidden from the Provider), and reviews you wrote remain public on the Provider’s profile, attributed to a “deleted user”, and continue to count towards the Provider’s rating.

Restoring your account. To restore your account, sign in with your existing credentials and verify a one-time code we email to your account address; verifying it re-activates the account with your data intact. We do not delete deactivated accounts automatically: the data is kept until you restore the account or ask us to remove your personal information completely.

Asking us to remove your personal information completely. Deleting your account in the app does not remove your personal information. To have it removed completely, contact our support team at contact@collaro.app or through the Contact us form in the app, from the email address or account concerned. We handle these requests manually: we may need to verify your identity first, and we will respond within the time required by applicable law. We will then delete your personal information, except for the records described in the next paragraph. You can ask us for a copy of your personal information in the same way. If you started signing up but did not finish (for example, you did not verify your email address or did not choose a role), the app does not let you delete that account yourself — contact us and we will delete it.

What we keep. Some records describe something that happened between you and another user, and are that person’s record as much as yours. To keep other users’ records complete and consistent, we keep past bookings and their invoices, reviews you wrote, messages you sent (which remain in the other person’s conversation), and reports you filed or that concern you, together with their evidence; to other users you appear in them only as a generic “deleted user”. We also keep our internal audit record of booking-, plan- and account-related events, which refers to accounts by internal identifiers, and we may retain other information where necessary to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent fraud and abuse, and enforce our agreements.

Retention generally. While your account is active, we retain your information for as long as needed to provide the Platform. Sign-in sessions expire automatically after a period of inactivity, and one-time codes expire shortly after they are sent. Bookings, reviews, messages, reports and the internal audit record are kept as part of the Platform’s records, including after an account is deleted in the app, as described above.
