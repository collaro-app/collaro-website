Deleting Your Data

Last updated: 24 September 2026

This page explains which of the things you create in Collaro you can delete or change on your own, without closing your account, and which records stay with us. It summarises, for convenience, what our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service say; where this page and those documents differ, those documents apply. To close your account, or to ask for your personal information to be removed completely, see Account Deletion.

1. Data You Can Delete or Change Yourself

  • Dogs (Owners). Open My Dogs from the side menu, tap the delete (bin) button on the dog’s card and confirm. The dog is deleted from your account. A dog cannot be deleted while it is part of a pending request or an accepted booking — including one that has not started yet; you can delete it once those bookings are completed, cancelled or rejected. The dog’s photo is deleted from our image host only if the dog was never included in a booking (see “What Is Kept Rather Than Removed” below).
  • Services (Providers). Open Services from the side menu, open the service and delete it. Before it is deleted you are shown what the deletion will affect: any pending requests for that service are cancelled automatically. A service cannot be deleted while it has accepted bookings that have not yet been completed — you can disable it in the meantime so that it stops taking new bookings (disabling it also cancels its pending requests).
  • Photos. Gallery images can be removed from the screen where you edit your provider profile, and the file is deleted from our image host. Your profile photo and a dog’s photo can be replaced with a new one, but not removed on their own. Replacing your profile photo replaces the stored file; a replaced dog photo is deleted unless the dog has been included in a booking.
  • Details you have written. Optional free-text fields, such as a dog’s notes, can be edited or cleared at any time from the same screens. A Provider’s biography can be edited but not left empty, because it is required for the profile to be listed.

2. Records You Cannot Delete in the App

Some records describe something that happened between you and another user, or are needed to keep the Platform safe. They are that person’s record as much as yours, so the app does not offer a way to delete them:

  • Bookings and invoices. A booking and its invoice are the record of an agreement between an Owner and a Provider, and are kept.
  • Reviews. Reviews currently cannot be edited or deleted after submission.
  • Messages. A message you have sent stays in the conversation for the person you sent it to.
  • Reports. A report you filed, and its evidence photos, cannot be withdrawn or deleted in the app.
  • Identity verification (Providers). The ID image and the selfie used for the automated check are never stored by us; we keep only the result, the reasons for any rejection, and the time of the check. That result is kept while your account is deactivated and is removed if you ask us to remove your personal information completely.

If you want your personal information removed completely, contact our support team as described in Account Deletion. Records like those above are kept so that other users’ records stay complete, but you appear in them only as a generic “deleted user”, and we may still need to retain information where necessary to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent fraud and abuse, and enforce our agreements.

3. What Is Kept Rather Than Removed

Two things in Collaro are not physically removed when they stop being visible, and we want to be precise about them:

  • Your account. Deleting your account in the app deactivates it and hides it on the Platform, but the data on it is kept so that you can restore the account later. See Account Deletion.
  • The details a booking keeps. When a booking request is sent, the app stores its own copy of the dogs’ and the service’s details as they were at that moment — on every booking, including requests that were later declined, expired or cancelled. That copy, including any health or medical notes, stays visible to the Provider as part of the booking. Editing or deleting the dog or the service afterwards does not change it, so a booking and its invoice still show what was actually booked. For the same reason, the photos of a dog that has been included in a booking are kept rather than deleted.

Where a retained record involves someone who has deleted their account, that person is shown as a generic “deleted user”.

4. What Deletion Cannot Reach

When you delete an image, we make a best effort to delete the corresponding file from our image host; copies may persist for a period in caches outside our control, and externally hosted images (such as a Google profile picture) are not stored or deleted by us.
